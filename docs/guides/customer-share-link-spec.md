# Customer Share Link (?from=) Specification

## Behavior
- When a dealer shares a product link, a `?from=<dealer_slug>` query parameter is attached.
- When visited, the store forces **Customer Mode** for all viewers, ensuring:
  1. No dealer wholesale prices or margins are visible.
  2. The dealer's store branding / business name appears as the referral partner.
  3. MRP is presented with official ReSTOR warranty badges.
