Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · Governing Restor (restor.in) & Shriji Domestic Appliances

1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Restor (restor.in), operated in collaboration with Shriji Domestic Appliances ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our users, dealers, retailers, and visitors across all digital platforms.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to fulfill dealer authentication, secure login, and customer support services:

  • Phone Numbers: Required for sending One-Time Passwords (OTPs) and account security notifications.
  • Store & Dealer Data: Business name, dealer name, city, GSTIN, and business email address for dealership onboarding.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, and session timestamps for cybersecurity and session protection.

3. WhatsApp Cloud API & OTP Authentication

We utilize the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API (provided by Meta Platforms, Inc.) to deliver authentication codes (OTPs) to your verified phone number:

  • No Spam: We never send unsolicited advertising or spam via WhatsApp.
  • Service Messages Only: Messages sent via WhatsApp are limited strictly to requested 6-digit verification codes, order confirmations, and essential account alerts.
  • User Consent: By entering your phone number and clicking "Request OTP", you expressly consent to receiving a verification code via WhatsApp.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We strictly do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information is only shared with authorized technology providers strictly to operate our platform:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: Data processor for sending transactional WhatsApp messages.
  • Zoho Corporation: For authorized dealer catalog and pricebook data synchronization.
  • Law Enforcement: Solely when mandated by applicable Indian laws or judicial requests.

5. User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform terms and data protection regulations, users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their personal information and account data:

  1. Send an email to hello@restor.in with the subject: Data Deletion Request.
  2. Provide your registered mobile number and business name.
  3. Our data compliance officer will process the request and permanently delete your phone number, session records, and account profile within 48 hours.
  4. You will receive an official written confirmation once your data has been completely erased.

6. Security

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is secured using SSL/TLS encryption. We enforce rigorous access controls to prevent unauthorized access or data leakage.
