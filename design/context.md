Interaction, Privacy & Data Integrity Dealer pricing: Only show the authenticated dealer's authorised price list. Enforce permissions server-side; hiding prices in the UI is not sufficient. Customer sharing: Public product links must never expose dealer prices, private schemes, internal notes, or restricted account data. Favourites: Persist across sessions where supported. Update counts and provide a helpful empty state. Schemes: Use real status, eligibility, validity, products, terms, and official PDFs. Never invent rewards or deadlines. Search: Search actual catalogue data and show honest no-results and error states. State & Accessibility Tokens State Treatment Default Selected Neutral surface, standard text, clear action hierarchy. Pale blue #EAF2FB with navy/blue icon or text emphasis. Success Warning Green #16834A plus a text label or icon; do not rely on colour alone. Amber #C98616 plus clear explanatory text. Error Loading Red #DC4545 with plain-language error and recovery action. Content-shaped skeletons or progress indicators; no indefinite fake success. Empty Offline Clear explanation and a relevant next action. Explain connectivity status and distinguish cached content from live-only data. Disabled Visibly unavailable and not actionable; explain requirements when useful. • Maintain accessible text contrast and readable type. • Use minimum 44 × 44 pt touch targets where practical. • Provide meaningful accessibility labels and screen-reader support. • Respect Reduce Motion and system accessibility preferences. • Make form validation and error recovery understandable. • Never imply an action succeeded until the underlying operation succeeds. 7. Quality Checklist One coherent design system across every screen. Consistent typography, colour, spacing, radii, icons, and button states. Correct ReSTOR logo and approved product/campaign assets. Real data only; no fabricated prices, counts, availability, reviews, or rewards. Customer-safe sharing and server-enforced dealer-price permissions. Stable bottom navigation that respects iOS safe areas. Loading, empty, error, offline, and permission-denied states designed. Responsive checks on smaller and larger iPhone screens. Prototype links and important interactions defined. Core principle: ReSTOR should be a daily business utility first and a promotional platform second. Optimise for finding a model, accessing the correct authorised price, understanding a scheme, sharing a safe product link, and getting support quickly. Page 5
